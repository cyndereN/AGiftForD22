# 致敬 D22 与 No Beijing 时代
# For D22, and the No Beijing years

（娄烨式粗粝手持镜头 + 相米慎二流动长镜头 + 地下噪音摇滚律动，沉浸式声音漫游 + 混音解谜）
(A rough handheld camera after Lou Ye, a drifting long take after Somai Shinji, the pulse of underground noise rock. A walk through sound, and a mix you solve.)

## 开场
## Opening

五道口，被一圈高校环绕的街上，坐落着一间名叫 D22 的小酒吧。
Wudaokou. On a street ringed by universities, a small bar called D22.

它在 2012 年正式歇业，不过这个名字，至今仍被大家口口相传。
It closed for good in 2012. The name, though, is still passed from mouth to mouth.

当年在北大任教的 Michael，亲眼见证了北京地下音乐圈蓬勃的生命力。他觉得，这座城市的声音，理应被全世界听见。
Michael, then teaching at Peking University, saw for himself how fiercely Beijing's underground was alive. He felt this city's sound deserved to be heard by the world.

他希望能够帮到那些尚且身处校园、富有天赋的音乐人，于是便有了 D22。
He wanted to help the gifted players still on campus. So there was D22.

这里很快就成了校园乐队的乐土。
It soon became a haven for campus bands.

刺猬乐队的子健那时还在北航念书，他回忆，演出结束后大伙总要喝酒，常常畅饮到天亮。他们把这叫作「坐穿馆」：在酒馆待到打烊，再换个地方继续欢聚。
Zijian of Hedgehog was still studying at Beihang. He remembers that after a show everyone drank, often until dawn. They called it zuo chuan guan: stay until the bar closes, then move on and keep the night going.

Carsick Cars、Snapline 都是从这里走出来的乐队，Carsick Cars 还曾经为传奇乐队 Sonic Youth 担任暖场嘉宾。
Carsick Cars and Snapline came out of this room. Carsick Cars once opened for Sonic Youth.

店里的酒水并不算出彩。
The drinks were nothing special.

珍贵的，是这间拥挤的小屋，以及那些毫无修饰、赤诚纯粹的声响。
What mattered was the crowded little room, and the sound left unadorned, plain and sincere.

## The record shop

唱片店，就是用来淘音乐的空间。黑胶唱片与 CD 整齐码放在格子之中，您抽出一张，就可以聆听它。
A record shop is a room for hunting down music. Vinyl and CDs stand in the bins. Pull one out, and you can listen.

北京如今依旧留存着这样的小店：
Beijing still keeps shops like this.

独音唱片 2011 年开设于鼓楼东大街，同时也运营唱片厂牌，痛仰、旅行团都曾和它结缘；
Duyin Records opened on Gulou East Street in 2011, and ran a label too. Miserable Faith and The Life Journey both passed through it.

福声唱片的资历更老，2002 年落户平安里，2018 年搬迁至冰窖口胡同。货架之上陈列着崔健、唐朝、黑豹，还有窦唯的作品。
Fusheng is older. It opened near Ping'anli in 2002 and moved to Bingjiaokou Hutong in 2018. The shelves hold Cui Jian, Tang Dynasty, Black Panther, and Dou Wei.

您此刻身处的，正是这样一间小店，不妨四处走走看一看。
You are in a shop like that now. Walk around and have a look.

### 老板

Eaten yet? Rushing off to the show?

门口那伙乐手，演出结束没有不喝上几杯的，还请您先别急着走。
The players by the door, none of them leave a show without a few drinks. Don't hurry off.

东西就在您身后，我刚刚放置好的，离开前麻烦您记得拿上。

## 酒
## The drink

酒，并非只为解渴。
Drink is not only for thirst.

当台上的声响轰然响起，人们总需要一点慰藉，来消解白日里的种种纷扰。
When the sound onstage breaks open, people need a little comfort, something to settle what the day stirred up.

尼采将其称作酒神精神。这并非神话传说，而是那一刻内心边界逐渐松弛，人声与躯体，随同音乐一同释放。
Nietzsche called it the Dionysian. Not a myth. The moment the inner edge loosens, and voice and body let go with the music.

所以看完演出总少不了小酌。音量放大了情绪，酒水将这份感受稳稳承接，留存于这一方小屋之内。
So a show is never without a drink. The volume turns the feeling up. The drink holds that feeling, and keeps it inside this small room.

D22 也是这般光景。演出落幕便坐穿酒馆，饮酒直至拂晓。
D22 was the same. When the set ended they sat the bar through, and drank until dawn.

杯中这一口，正是噪音尚未消散之时，留给人们的余韵。
This mouthful is the aftertaste left while the noise has not yet gone.

还请您拿好酒杯，这是您要熟悉的第二件事。
Take the glass. This is the second thing you will come to know.

### 酒杯交互
### The glass

情绪放大器，还请您切莫贪杯。
An amplifier for feeling. Please don't drink past your limit.

### The question

噪音已然将情绪推至高点。喝下这一口，请问您希望带来怎样的感受？
The noise has already pushed the feeling to its height. This mouthful. What do you want it to do?

- **Turn it up again**

- **Hold it in this room**
BGM：乐曲收敛，房间环境声响大过失真，低音沉稳，仿佛您依旧安坐在酒馆之中；
The mix: the song dries out, the room louder than the distortion, the low end steady, as if you were still sitting in the bar.
相机：抖动轻微，视野固定 60，转向节奏放缓，使用长镜头缓缓铺展画面；

The picture: a little sharper, contrast and saturation down, a wide soft vignette, bloom off.

- **把白天关掉**
- **Switch the day off**
BGM：鼓点向远处退去，混响增强，余下低频与电流的嗡鸣；
The mix: the drums retreat, the reverb grows, what remains is low end and the hum of current.
相机：大多时候保持静止，偶尔剧烈晃动后骤然定格，视野收至 52；
The camera: still most of the time, then one hard shake and a sudden hold, the view pulled in to 52.
后处理：降低画面亮度，保留对比度，色调清冷寡淡，暗角浓重。
The picture: darker, the contrast kept, a cold thin color, a heavy vignette.

## 胡同口
## The mouth of the hutong

酒意还萦绕在喉头，我们来到了胡同。还请您先移步到那扇门前。
The drink is still in the throat. Here is the hutong. Please walk to that door first.

### 门
### The door

已满。
Full.

部分 Livehouse 会开放即兴 jam，乐器就位，空位尚存，任何人都可以上前参与合奏。
Some livehouses open the floor for a jam. The instruments are set, a seat is free, and anyone can step in and play along.

也有些场地在邀约乐手登台，并非人人都可上场。他们需要对应乐器的演奏者：吉他、鼓、贝斯，匹配到位，门才会为您敞开。
Other rooms are inviting players onstage. Not everyone may go up. They need the player of one instrument: guitar, drums, bass. Match it, and the door opens.

若想要入场，既需要些许音乐素养，也需要一份胆量。
To get in, you need a little musical sense, and some nerve.

### 鸽子
### The pigeons

天上飞过一群鸽子，请您捉住其中一只，它的尾巴系着鸽哨。
A flock is passing overhead. Catch one. A pigeon whistle is tied to its tail.

老北京习惯在房顶饲养鸽子，鸽哨大多是小小的葫芦，风灌入其中，便会发出鸣响。
Old Beijing kept pigeons on the roof. The whistle is usually a little gourd. Wind pours in, and it sounds.

并非单只飞鸟在鸣叫，成群掠过之时，每一只的哨音各不相同，交织在一起，化作城市上空的和弦。
It is not one bird calling. When the flock sweeps past, each whistle is different, and woven together they become a chord over the city.

这份声响，和市井叫卖属于同一类。胡同不靠扩音喇叭，依靠的是飞鸟振翅，与人的嗓音。
This sound belongs with the street cries. A hutong does not rely on a loudspeaker. It relies on wings, and on a human voice.

### 蛐蛐
### The cricket

请您拨开草丛，里面藏着一只蛐蛐。
Part the grass. A cricket is hiding inside.

秋日胡同，少不了它的鸣唱。人们从砖缝、草丛中将它捕捉回来，养在陶罐之内。
An autumn hutong is never without this song. People catch them from the cracks in the brick and from the grass, and keep them in a clay jar.

斗蛐蛐可不是玩乐消遣。两只蛐蛐置于盆中，以草茎轻轻挑逗，比拼的是缠斗，也是鸣叫。
Cricket fighting is not a pastime. Two of them in a bowl, a grass stem to tease them. What is weighed is the grapple, and the song.

明清时期人们便喜爱这般声响，宫廷之中听，胡同街巷也听。纤细的鸣音，比鸽哨更加贴近，仿佛就在耳畔。
People loved this sound in the Ming and the Qing. They listened at court, and in the hutong lanes. A thin note, closer than the pigeon whistle, as if it were against the ear.

### 磨剪子磨刀
### Sharpening scissors, sharpening knives

胡同深处传来吆喝：磨剪子嘞，戗菜刀。
From deep in the hutong, a cry: sharpen the scissors, sharpen the knife.

这是老北京传统的市井货声。匠人沿街走过，住户便会把剪刀菜刀拿出来打磨。
This is an old Beijing street cry. The sharpener comes down the lane, and the houses bring out their scissors and kitchen knives.

磨石沾水，一下一下往复打磨。吆喝本身便是乐器，告知整条街巷：可以打磨厨具了。
A whetstone, a little water, stroke after stroke. The cry itself is the instrument. It tells the whole lane: time to sharpen.

鸽哨响彻天际，蛐蛐鸣于草丛，吆喝回荡在路上。这三者，都属于此地独有的音乐。
The pigeon whistle fills the sky. The cricket sings in the grass. The cry hangs on the road. All three belong to this place alone.

### 问
### The question

这里有两种声音：鸽哨与叫卖出自人手，蛐蛐的鸣叫源于它本身。请问您更偏爱哪一种？
There are two kinds of sound here. The pigeon whistle and the vendor's cry are made by hand. The cricket's song is its own. Which do you prefer?

- **人为**
- **Made by people**
BGM：鸽哨与叫卖声前置，磨刀石的声响化作节拍，蛐蛐鸣响融入节奏，不再肆意鸣叫；
The mix: pigeon whistle and the cry in front, the whetstone turned into a beat, the cricket folded into that beat, no longer calling as it pleases.
相机：带有刻意的手持抖动，视野收至 56，转向平稳，缓缓对准门、飞鸽、吆喝之人；
The camera: a deliberate handheld shake, the view pulled to 56, the turn steady, slowly finding the door, the birds, the crier.
后处理：边缘锐度提升，对比度略高，整体暖调，飞鸟带有淡淡光晕。
The picture: sharper edges, a little more contrast, a warm cast, a faint bloom on the birds.

- **自然**
- **Natural**
BGM：振翅声、蛐蛐鸣唱居于前景，叫卖声退至胡同远处，没有固定节拍，随心作响；
The mix: wings and the cricket in front, the cry fallen back down the hutong, no fixed beat, each sound as it comes.
相机：几乎无抖动，视野放宽至 68，转向舒缓，天空与草丛一同收纳进画面；

后处理：画面质感柔和，饱和度降低，草木绿意，暗角浅淡，关闭光晕。

- **都不喜欢**

BGM：鸽哨、叫卖、蛐蛐声一同远去，余下墙壁与风声，偶尔一声异响转瞬消散；
The mix: whistle, cry, and cricket all recede. Walls and wind remain. Now and then a stray sound, gone at once.
相机：细碎不停抖动，没有明确注视方向，视野固定 60；
The camera: a fine shake that will not stop, no clear thing to look at, the view fixed at 60.
后处理：色调偏向灰白，对比度硬朗，暗角收紧，画面清晰，却难言悦目。
The picture: a gray-white cast, hard contrast, the vignette tight. Clear, and not pleasant to look at.

## Livehouse

Livehouse，是乐队站在您面前表演的空间。它不是音乐厅，也并非顺带播放音乐的舞厅。
A livehouse is a room where the band stands in front of you and plays. It is not a concert hall. It is not a dance hall that happens to have music on.

酒吧里的演出亦是如此，一旁设有吧台，酒水与舞台同在。各位前来，都是为了台上的表演者。
A show in a bar is the same night, with a counter beside it. The drinks are there, and so is the stage. Everyone came for the people on it.

所谓乐队文化，便是几个人，几样乐器，若干尚未完成的作品。预约一场演出，或是坐下参与即兴合奏。排练仅仅只是练习，唯有在这样的场地完成表演，才算真正成就一支乐队。
Band culture is a few people, a few instruments, songs that are not finished yet. Book a show, or sit in and play along. Rehearsal is only practice. Play it through in a room like this, and it is a band.

2005 年的 D22，便是属于北京的 CBGB。
D22 in 2005 was Beijing's CBGB.

当年资金十分拮据，经费大多投入音响设备，电力系统与洗手间便无力顾及。演出时常中途断电，甚至出现积水漫溢。可 D22 的热闹，从不会因条件简陋就此停歇。
Money was tight. Most of it went into the PA. The power and the toilets had to wait. Shows often died halfway when the lights went out, or when the water rose. The noise of D22 never stopped for the roughness.

对于乐手与乐迷而言，D22 是一处乌托邦。Michael 创办这里，本就不谋求盈利。演出收入，要么用于维护音响，要么交付登台的音乐人。到场的大多是囊中羞涩的高校学子，门票仅三十至五十元。
For players and listeners, D22 was a utopia. Michael did not found it to make money. What the door took went back into the PA, or to the people who played. Most of the crowd were students with little in their pockets. A ticket was thirty to fifty yuan.

善待乐手，也善待听众，它的名声就此传开。前来演出的乐队络绎不绝，观众也越来越多。后期几乎场场满座，十点半便必须闭店限流。
Kind to the players, and kind to the listeners, the name traveled. Bands kept coming. The crowd kept growing. Later, almost every night was full. At ten-thirty the door had to shut.

### 三大件
### The three

组建一支乐队，先要备齐三样乐器：鼓、吉他、贝斯。主唱可以后续加入。三者不全，那依旧只算是排练。
To start a band, first gather three instruments: drums, guitar, bass. The singer can come later. Without all three, it is still only a rehearsal.

- **鼓**：掌管时间。一次敲击，整个空间便拥有了当下。它不承载旋律，让所有人同步向前。震动先抵达胸腔，之后才传入耳朵。
- **Drums**: they keep the time. One strike, and the whole room has a now. They carry no melody. They move everyone forward together. The vibration reaches the chest first, and the ear after.

- **吉他**：弥漫在空气之中的一层情绪。和弦、噪音，一句未曾唱出的心里话。在这样的场地，吉他常常使用失真效果。干净音色留作私下练习，登上舞台，要让墙壁一同震颤共鸣。
- **Guitar**: a layer of feeling hung in the air. Chords, noise, a sentence of the heart not yet sung. In a room like this the guitar is usually distorted. The clean tone stays for practice at home. Onstage, the walls have to shake with it.

- **贝斯**：音高最低，也最容易被忽略。若是去掉贝斯，鼓依旧敲打，吉他依旧鸣响，整首乐曲却摇摇欲坠。它把鼓的节奏和吉他的音符紧紧联结。与其说听见贝斯，不如说脚下切实感受到了根基。
- **Bass**: the lowest, and the easiest to miss. Take it away and the drums still hit, the guitar still rings, and the song cannot stand. It binds the drum's time to the guitar's notes. You do not so much hear it as feel the floor under your feet.

### 舞台
### The stage

三大乐器已然就位，请您踏上这座小小的舞台。
The three instruments are in place. Please step onto this small stage.

灯光亮起的那一刻，这间小屋，便快要承载不住奔涌的情绪。
The moment the lights come up, this little room can barely hold the feeling rushing through it.

### 问
### The question

灯光落在您的脸上，恍惚间台下仿佛汇聚万人，可您依旧是您。
The light falls on your face. For a moment it seems ten thousand people are gathered below. You are still you.

请问您站上舞台，是为了什么？
What did you come up here for?

- **被记住**：名字可以比场地活得更久。2012 年酒吧关闭，可不少名字依旧被人们谈起，这便是您所求。
- **To be remembered**: a name can outlive the room. The bar closed in 2012, and still some names are spoken. This is what you want.

- **把这一首唱完**：台下万人也好，区区三十人也罢。乐曲既然开启，便应当好好收尾，您只求完整唱完这一曲。
- **To finish this song**: ten thousand below, or only thirty. A piece that has started ought to be brought to its end. You only want to sing this one through.

- **不知道**：不少人登台之时也并无答案。只听见鼓点已然响起，不妨先站在这里。
- **I don't know**: plenty of people have no answer when they step up. What they hear is that the drum has already started. Stand here first.

### 万人现场
### Ten thousand

有些乐队憧憬体育场的演出，万众齐声合唱。副歌响起之时，仿佛整座城市都在随同歌唱。
Some bands dream of the stadium, ten thousand voices on the chorus. When it comes back, it feels as if the whole city is singing along.

灯光放大乐队的名字，面孔模糊不清，乐声却能够抵达最后一排，飘出场馆门外。
The lights enlarge the name. The faces blur. The sound still reaches the last row, and drifts out past the door.

也有不少人不愿改变。地下音乐偏爱 Livehouse 那份滚烫的躁动。汗水、酒气、失真音效贴着后颈，您可以看清谁在点头，谁闭上双眼沉浸其中。
Others will not change. The underground prefers the heat of a livehouse. Sweat, drink, distortion against the back of the neck. You can see who is nodding, and whose eyes are shut.

狭小的现场是贴身的狂热。音响震耳，地板晃动，稍有失误全场都听得清清楚楚，那份热烈近在咫尺。体育场将这份狂热铺展开来，化作笼罩所有人的氛围。
A small room is a fever against the skin. The PA is too loud, the floor moves, and if a line goes wrong everyone hears it. That heat stays close. A stadium spreads the fever out, until it becomes the weather over everyone.

D22 从未变成大型体育场。它所珍视的，正是这间容纳不下更多人的小屋。十点半关门，只因场内早已满员。
D22 never became a stadium. What it kept was the room that could not hold any more. At ten-thirty the door shut, because the room was already full.

可梦想从未消散。带着三件乐器站上小舞台的人，下一刻，便能够置身盛大的夜色之中。
But the dream does not leave. Someone who carries the three onto a small stage stands, in the next moment, in a very large night.

此刻，就是那个瞬间。万众瞩目，灯光朝向您。
This is that moment. Ten thousand. The lights are on you.

## 演唱完
## After the song

最后一个音符缓缓落下。
The last note settles.

一曲终了，灯光便可暗下。
The song is over. The lights can go down.

场地回归原本的模样，它所能容纳的，本就只有这么多。
